## Reflective Journal

For this week's conditionals prototype, I decided to make some complex animations by building on my previous soccer projects. First, I intended to create an animation of **two players passing the ball back and forth** by using conditionals. Second, I made an animation of a player **running towards the ball and shooting it into the goal**. Lastly, I created a **corner kick animation**. While working on this assignment, I encountered some obstacles and realized this work is not easy for my current skill set. As a result, each of my projects had some minor problems. Therefore, I decided to seek help from the teacher in the lab. Thanks to their guidance, I managed to solve these problems on time.
