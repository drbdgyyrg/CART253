## Reflective Journal

I feel this project is hard because my p5js skill set has so many limitation. Originally, I was trying to do a **strawberry** but I could not make a shape of strawberry body with curvature. I did research online and tried **BezierVertex** but it didn't work. After that I changed my idea and did a soccer field; it is a much easier process but the difficulty is the accuracy. Therefore, I did data research of soccer field such as the size of penalty box, goal, arc on so on. And then I calculated those numbers and filled in my Javascript. I feel such an accomplishment by doing it.
