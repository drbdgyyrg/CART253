## Reflective Journal

I used one of my prototypes from last week as a baseline and expanded upon it by adding animations. For the **first** variable prototype, I changed the turf color from green to yellow, which was relatively straightforward. For the **second** version, I used circles to represent players, animating a winger crossing the ball to a striker. Initially, I wanted to animate two players passing the ball back and forth, but it proved too complex for my current skill set. A similar issue occurred with my **third** prototype; I intended to animate a player running toward the ball and shooting it into the goal, but I have not yet mastered the necessary timing. Consequently, I opted for a simpler animation. This assignment made me realize the limitations of my current technical skills, and I plan to progressively expand them through future studies and online research.
